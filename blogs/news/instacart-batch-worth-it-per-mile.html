Is This Instacart Batch Worth It? Pay Per Mile and Real Hourly (2026)

Short answer: take the batch pay, subtract 76 cents for every mile (the 2026 IRS rate from July 1), then divide by the hours the batch takes, shopping included. A $14 batch with 9 miles that takes 50 minutes pays about $8.59 an hour.

Why shopping time changes everything

Delivery-only apps are mostly driving. An Instacart batch adds the time in the store: walking aisles, waiting in line, swapping items. Two batches with the same pay per mile can pay very different hourly rates.

The quick check before you accept

  1. Pay per mile under about $1.50? The car takes half or more of it.
  2. Guess the minutes, store time included. Divide what is left after miles by the hours.
  3. Count the drive back if the drop-off leaves you somewhere with no orders.

The miles are also your deduction

Every business mile you log cuts your tax. 10,000 business miles in 2026, half before and half after July 1, is a $7,425 deduction at the IRS standard rates - but only with a log made at or near the time.
