Grubhub Driver Pay Per Mile 2026: Real Pay After Miles and Taxes

Short answer: Grubhub does not pay a set rate per mile. Each offer shows one estimated total, built from the expected time and distance plus any tip, so your real pay per mile is that total divided by every mile the order costs you, including the drive back to where the orders are. At the 2026 IRS rate of 76 cents a mile, an order under about $1.25 a mile leaves very little once tax is set aside. How Grubhub pay works, in one paragraph

Grubhub drivers are independent contractors. Each offer shows an estimated payout before you accept it: a base amount figured from the estimated time and distance of the delivery, plus the customer's tip, which drivers keep in full. Bonuses and missions can add to that on some days. What the offer does not show is the drive back to the restaurants after a long drop-off, or the wait at a slow kitchen. Those miles and minutes cost you the same as paid ones, which is why the number on the screen always looks better than the one in your bank.

Step 2: the real hour after the car and tax

The IRS standard mileage rate is its estimate of the full cost of driving a mile: gas, oil, repairs, tires, insurance, registration and depreciation. For 2026 it is 72.5 cents through June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. Each offer below is charged 76 cents a mile, then self-employment tax (15.3% of 92.35% of profit) and 12% income tax on profit after half the self-employment tax.

These numbers are honest, not pessimistic: the car really does cost about that much per mile over its life, you just pay it later in repairs, tires and a lower trade-in. If your car is cheaper to run than the IRS rate, your real hour is better than this table; if it costs more, it is worse.

A 20-second check before you accept

  1. Add the drive back. Offer miles plus the miles from the drop-off to where orders come from.
  2. Divide the pay by those miles. Under $1.25 a mile, the car takes more than half at 76 cents.
  3. Check the hour. A short, well-paid order at a slow restaurant can still be a bad hour.

The miles are also your biggest tax deduction

Every one of those miles, the empty ones included, is deductible if you log it: date, miles and purpose, written at or near the time. At 76 cents, 200 logged miles a week for 40 weeks is $6,080.00 off your profit, which saves about $859.08 in self-employment tax alone. The drive from home to your first order and back home at the end are where the commuting rules come in (IRS Topic 510), so log them as their own line.

FAQ

How much does Grubhub pay per mile?

There is no set per-mile rate. Each offer shows an estimated total from time, distance and tip. Divide it by all the miles the order costs you, drive back included, to get your real number.

Does Grubhub pay for gas or mileage?

Distance is part of how the base amount is estimated, but there is no separate gas payment. You pay for the car, which is why logged miles are a tax deduction.

Do Grubhub drivers keep their tips?

Yes, drivers keep 100% of customer tips. Tips are taxable income; log them apart from base pay for the federal tips deduction.

Do Grubhub drivers pay taxes?

Yes. Grubhub drivers are independent contractors, so no tax is withheld. You owe self-employment tax once profit reaches $400 for the year, plus income tax, and may need quarterly estimated payments.
