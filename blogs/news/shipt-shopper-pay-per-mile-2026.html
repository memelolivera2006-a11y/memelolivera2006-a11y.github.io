How Shipt pay works, in one paragraph

Shipt says shopper pay has three parts: offer pay, bonuses and tips. The offer card shows an estimated pay before you accept, built on an effort-based model, and that estimate is the minimum you are guaranteed for completing the order. You keep 100% of tips; a tip added late can land in the next pay period. Shipt also says pay won't fall below its earnings standard of at least $16 an hour, but that is based on Shipt's own effort and time estimates, and it is before gas, the car's wear and self-employment tax.

Step 2: the real hour after the car and tax

The IRS standard mileage rate is its estimate of the full cost of driving a mile: gas, oil, repairs, tires, insurance, registration and depreciation. For 2026 it is 72.5 cents through June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. A 20-second check before you accept

  1. Add the drive back. Store, customer, and home or the next store.
  2. Divide the pay by those miles. Under $1.25 a mile, the car takes more than half at 76 cents.
  3. Guess the shopping time honestly. A 60-item order at a busy store can turn a good mile into a bad hour.

FAQ

How much does Shipt pay per mile?

There is no per-mile rate. Each offer shows an estimated total based on Shipt's effort and time estimate, plus tips. Divide it by all the miles the order costs you, drive back included, to get your real number.

Is Shipt's $16 an hour guaranteed?

Shipt says pay won't fall below its earnings standard of at least $16 an hour, based on its own effort and time estimates. It is measured before your car costs and taxes, so what you keep is lower.

Do Shipt shoppers keep their tips?

Yes, Shipt says shoppers receive 100% of tips on top of order pay. Tips are taxable income.

Do Shipt shoppers pay taxes?

Yes. Shipt shoppers are independent contractors, so no tax is withheld. You owe self-employment tax once profit reaches $400 for the year, plus income tax, and may need quarterly estimated payments.
