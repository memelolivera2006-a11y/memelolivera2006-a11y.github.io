Why 76 cents a mile

The IRS standard mileage rate is its estimate of what a business mile costs: gas, wear, maintenance, insurance and depreciation. For 2026 it is 72.5 cents through June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. Your own car may cost more or less, but the miles are never free.

A full-time week

40 hours, $1,200.00 gross, 1,000 miles: the miles cost $760.00 at the IRS rate, leaving $440.00, or $11.00 an hour. These are example numbers - put your own in.

Log every mile - it is also your deduction

The miles that eat your hourly are deductible if you can prove them. The IRS wants a log made at or near the time: date, miles and purpose. That 1,000-mile week is $760.00 of deductions after July 1.
