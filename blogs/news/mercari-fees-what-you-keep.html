Mercari Fees 2026: 10% Selling Fee, What You Keep

Short answer: Mercari takes 10% of the item price plus any shipping the buyer pays. There is no separate payment processing fee. On a $25 sale you keep $22.50.

Skip the math: the Reseller Profit Tracker ($17, Excel + Google Sheets) does it for you: every platform's fees taken out of each sale, real profit per item.

The figures use US seller rates as checked in October 2026, on the item price alone, with no promoted listings, no store subscription and no shipping. Platforms change their fees, so check each one's own fee page before you price anything.

Mercari's fees since January 2025

  • Selling fee: 10% of the item price and buyer-paid shipping, for listings created or updated since 6 January 2025.
  • No payment processing fee for those listings.
  • Cashing out: direct deposit is free; Instant Pay costs $3 per cash-out.
  • Cancellations: frequent cancelling can cost 5% of the item price, capped at $25.

You will still see older articles quoting 12.9% + $0.50 or a buyer-pays model. Mercari's own help page (updated May 2026) describes the 10% fee above.

What you keep is not all yours

Reselling profit is usually taxable income, and the IRS taxes the profit, not the sale price. Many resellers move a fixed share of every payout, often 15% to 30%, into a separate account so April is not a shock. This is not tax advice. Ask a tax professional what rate fits you.

Profit means sales minus what the items cost you, and only the items you sold count: see cost of goods sold for resellers, worked out.
