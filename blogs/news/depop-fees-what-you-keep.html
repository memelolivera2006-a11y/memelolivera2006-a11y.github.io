Depop Fees 2026: No Selling Fee, 3.3% + $0.45 (US)

Short answer: US Depop sellers pay no selling fee, only payment processing of 3.3% + $0.45. On a $25 sale you keep $23.72. Boost a listing and a 12% fee is added if it sells through the boost.

The figures use Depop's US fees as published on its help center (checked October 2026), on the item price alone with no shipping or tax. Platforms change their fees, so check Depop's own fee page before you price anything.

Depop's fees for US sellers

  • Selling fee: none for sellers based in the US, UK and Australia.
  • Payment processing: 3.3% + $0.45 per sale, charged on the item price plus shipping and any taxes.
  • Boosted Listings: 12% of the item price and any shipping you arrange, only if a buyer saw or tapped the boosted listing and bought within 28 days.

You will still see older articles quoting a 10% Depop fee. Depop dropped the selling fee for US sellers in 2024; the 10% now only applies outside the UK, US and Australia.

Is boosting worth 12%?

Only if it sells items that would otherwise sit. Boosting a $25 item costs $3.00 when it sells through the boost. If that item would have sold within a few weeks anyway, the boost cost you $3.00 of profit. Track days to sell before and after you boost and you will know.

What you keep is not all yours

Reselling profit is usually taxable income, and the IRS taxes the profit, not the sale price. Many resellers move a fixed share of every payout, often 15% to 30%, into a separate account so April is not a shock. This is not tax advice. Ask a tax professional what rate fits you.
