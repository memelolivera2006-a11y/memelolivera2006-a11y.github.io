How Much Does eBay Take? 2026 Fees on $5 to $200 Sales

Short answer: on a $20 sale in most categories, eBay takes $3.12 and you keep $16.88. That is the 13.6% final value fee plus a $0.40 per-order fee.

The figures use US seller rates as checked in October 2026, on the item price alone, with no promoted listings, no store subscription and no shipping. Platforms change their fees, so check each one's own fee page before you price anything.

The two fees on most eBay sales

  • Final value fee: 13.6% of the total the buyer pays in most categories. That total includes the shipping you charge and the sales tax eBay collects, so the real fee is a little higher than 13.6% of your item price.
  • Per-order fee: $0.40, or $0.30 when the order is $10 or less.

Some categories differ (books, music and some collectibles are higher; sneakers over $150 and guitars are lower), and a Store subscription lowers the rate. Promoted Listings are charged on top.

The flat per-order fee is why cheap items hurt: a $5 sale loses almost 20% to fees, a $200 sale under 14%.

The fee is not your only cost

A $20 sale that you bought for $6 and shipped for $5.50 leaves $5.38 before tax, not $16.88. Most resellers know their sale price and their fee. Far fewer track the buy price and shipping label on every single item, which is where the money quietly goes.

What you keep is not all yours

Reselling profit is usually taxable income, and the IRS taxes the profit, not the sale price.
