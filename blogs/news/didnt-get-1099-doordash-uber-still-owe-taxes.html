Didn't Get a 1099 From DoorDash or Uber? You May Still Owe (2026)

Short answer: for 2026 pay, apps only have to send a 1099-NEC once they pay you $2,000 or more, and a 1099-K only above $20,000 and 200 transactions. Plenty of part-time drivers get no form. You still report every dollar, and you owe self-employment tax once your net profit reaches $400.

Why no form showed up

  • 1099-NEC threshold for payments made in 2026: $2,000 (it was $600). It is adjusted for inflation from 2027.
  • 1099-K threshold: more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions. Both must be true.
  • Some states use lower thresholds, so you may get a state form anyway.

The thresholds decide when the app must report you to the IRS. They do not decide whether the money is taxable. It is.

The number that matters: $400 of profit

If your net earnings from self-employment are $400 or more, you file Schedule SE and pay self-employment tax, even with no 1099 and even if you owe no income tax. Profit is pay plus tips minus your mileage deduction and other expenses, so the miles decide which side of $400 you land on.

How to get your numbers without a 1099

  • Open the tax section of each app's driver dashboard and download the yearly summary. Most show pay, tips and on-app miles even when no form is issued.
  • Add any cash tips and bonuses the summary leaves out.
  • Total your own business miles by half-year: 72.5 cents before July 1, 76 cents from July 1. On-app miles usually miss the drive to your first pickup and back home from the last drop-off. Whether those count depends on your setup, so log them and ask your preparer.
  • Report everything on Schedule C as business income.

Don't forget the January payment

If you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year, the fourth estimated payment is due January 15, 2027. If you have a W-2 job too, raising your withholding there can cover it instead.
