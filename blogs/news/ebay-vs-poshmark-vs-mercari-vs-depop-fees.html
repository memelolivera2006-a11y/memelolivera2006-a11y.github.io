eBay vs Poshmark vs Mercari vs Depop Fees (2026 Comparison)

Short answer: on a $50 item, Depop leaves you $47.90, Mercari $45.00, eBay $42.80 and Poshmark $40.00. Lowest fee is not the same as most money, though: the platform where your item sells fastest at the best price usually wins.

The figures use US seller rates as checked in October 2026, on the item price alone, with no promoted listings, no store subscription and no shipping. Platforms change their fees, so check each one's own fee page before you price anything.

How to read this

  • Under $15, Poshmark's $2.95 flat fee is brutal. Small items belong on Mercari, Depop or local.
  • Depop keeps the most at almost every price, but only if your item is the kind Depop buyers want (streetwear, vintage, Y2K).
  • eBay sits in the middle on fees and has the most buyers by far, which often means a higher sale price that more than covers the difference.
  • The real comparison is profit per item: sale price minus fee, buy price and shipping. A $40 eBay sale can beat a $32 Depop sale even after fees.

What you keep is not all yours

Reselling profit is usually taxable income, and the IRS taxes the profit, not the sale price. Many resellers move a fixed share of every payout, often 15% to 30%, into a separate account so April is not a shock. When is reselling taxable, and the 2026 1099-K rule. This is not tax advice.
