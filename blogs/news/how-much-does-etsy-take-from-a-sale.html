How Much Does Etsy Take? Etsy Fees on $5–$100 Sales (2026)

Short answer: on a $10 digital item, Etsy takes $1.40 and you keep $8.60. If that sale came through Etsy's Offsite Ads, Etsy takes $2.90 and you keep $7.10.

The figures below use Etsy's US seller rates as checked in September 2026. They assume a digital download with no shipping, and they leave out sales tax. Rates change, so check Etsy's own fee page before you price anything.

The four fees on every Etsy sale

  • Listing fee: $0.20, charged when you list and again each time an item sells or renews.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the sale price (plus shipping and gift wrap, if you charge them).
  • Payment processing: 3% + $0.25 per order (US rate).
  • Offsite Ads: 15%, but only on sales that came from an Etsy ad on Google, Instagram and other sites. Shops over $10,000 a year pay 12% and cannot opt out.

The fixed $0.45 (listing plus the processing flat fee) hurts most on cheap items. That is why a $5 product loses more than 18% to fees while a $100 product loses 10%.

The number Etsy does not show you

What you keep is still not yours to spend. Side-hustle profit is usually taxable income. Many sellers move a fixed share of each payout, often 15% to 30%, into a separate account so the tax bill does not come as a shock. At 15%, the $8.60 from a $10 sale means setting aside $1.29.
