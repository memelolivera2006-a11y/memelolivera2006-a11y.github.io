Quarterly Taxes for Uber, DoorDash and Instacart Drivers (2026 Dates a

Short answer: if you drive for Uber, Lyft, DoorDash or Instacart and expect to owe $1,000 or more in tax for 2026, the IRS expects you to pay during the year, in four estimated payments. The 2026 due dates are April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027.

Why gig drivers owe more than they expect

No one withholds tax from app payouts. On top of income tax, you pay self-employment tax: 15.3% (12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare) on 92.35% of your net earnings. Net means after business expenses, and for most drivers the biggest expense by far is the miles.

Same driver, same year: the log is worth $1,049.12 in self-employment tax alone, before income tax. The deduction uses the 2026 IRS rates: 72.5 cents a mile through June 30 and 76 cents from July 1 (5,000 x $0.725 + 5,000 x $0.76 = $7,425). These are example numbers - put your own in.

How to set aside the right amount

  1. After every week, move a fixed share of your payouts to a separate account. Many drivers use 25-30% of net to cover self-employment tax plus income tax; your real rate depends on your bracket.
  2. Log miles as you drive. The deduction is only as good as your record: date, miles and business purpose, written at or near the time.
  3. Pay on the four dates with Form 1040-ES or IRS Direct Pay. Paying late can add a penalty even if you pay in full in April.
