Gumroad vs Etsy Fees 2026: What You Keep Per Sale

Short answer: on your own link, Gumroad and Etsy keep almost the same amount. A $20.00 file keeps $17.65 on Etsy and $17.50 on Gumroad. The big differences are the two marketplace fees: Etsy's Offsite Ads (15% when an ad brought the buyer) and Gumroad Discover (30% when Gumroad's own marketplace brought the buyer).

The fees, side by side

  • Etsy: $0.20 listing fee + 6.5% transaction fee + 3% + $0.25 payment processing, on every sale. Plus 15% Offsite Ads on the sales an Etsy ad brought in.
  • Gumroad: 10% + $0.50 on sales through your own profile or links. 30% on sales that came from Gumroad Discover. No monthly fee.

On a $20.00 file the gap between Etsy and Gumroad on your own link is $0.15 - Etsy keeps more. Etsy keeps a few cents more at every price here. The fixed fees ($0.45 on Etsy, $0.50 on Gumroad) hurt cheap files most: at $5.00, Etsy keeps 82% and Gumroad 80%.

The real difference is traffic, not fees

Etsy's fee buys you a marketplace where people search for planners and spreadsheets. Gumroad's 10% buys you checkout, file delivery and nothing else - every buyer has to come from your own posts, email or site. The marketplace extras (Offsite Ads, Discover) cost the most but only apply to buyers the platform found for you.

A month of 50 sales at $10.00

Etsy keeps $430.00; Gumroad on your own link keeps $425.00. Before income tax - set some aside every month.
