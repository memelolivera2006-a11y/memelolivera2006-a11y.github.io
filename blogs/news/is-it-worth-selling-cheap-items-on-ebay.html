Is It Worth Selling Cheap Items on eBay? $5-$20 Flip Math (2026)

Short answer: it can be, but the fixed fees hit cheap items hardest. On eBay an $8 sale keeps $6.61; if the item cost you $3 at the thrift store, that is $3.61 for the sourcing, photos, listing and the trip to the post office. The same $3 item listed at $15 keeps $9.56.

eBay takes 13.6% plus 30 cents on orders of $10 or less (40 cents above). Mercari takes 10%. Poshmark takes a flat $2.95 under $15 and 20% from $15 up, which is why a $12 Poshmark sale keeps only $9.05.

Three rules for cheap flips

  1. Price for the fees. The jump from $8 to $15 more than doubles what a $3 item leaves.
  2. Bundle the small stuff. One $20 lot pays the per-order fee once instead of four times.
  3. Know your app's line. Under $15, Poshmark's flat $2.95 is the most expensive option on this list; Mercari keeps the most at every price here.
