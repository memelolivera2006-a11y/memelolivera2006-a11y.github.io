Poshmark Fees 2026: What You Keep on $5 to $200 Sales

Short answer: Poshmark takes a flat $2.95 on sales under $15 and 20% on sales of $15 or more. On a $25 sale you keep $20.00.

The figures use US seller rates as checked in October 2026, on the item price alone, with no promoted listings, no store subscription and no shipping. Platforms change their fees, so check each one's own fee page before you price anything.

How Poshmark's fee works

  • Under $15: $2.95 flat. On a $5 item that is 59% of the sale.
  • $15 and up: 20%. No listing fee, no processing fee, and the buyer pays shipping with a prepaid label.

The $14 vs $15 trap

A $14 sale keeps $11.05. A $15 sale keeps $12.00. Pricing at exactly $15 earns you 95 cents more than $14, but pricing at $16 only keeps $12.80. Below $15 every dollar of price is a full dollar to you; above it you keep 80 cents of each extra dollar.

Poshmark takes the biggest cut of the four at every price on this table. Sellers still use it because the buyers are there, and a sale that happens beats a cheaper fee on one that does not.

What you keep is not all yours

Reselling profit is usually taxable income, and the IRS taxes the profit, not the sale price. Many resellers move a fixed share of every payout, often 15% to 30%, into a separate account so April is not a shock. This is not tax advice. Ask a tax professional what rate fits you.
