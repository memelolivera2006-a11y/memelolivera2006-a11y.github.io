Whatnot Fees 2026: 8% + 2.9% + $0.30, New Tiers Explained

Short answer: most US Whatnot sellers pay 8% commission on the item price plus 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing. On a $25 sale you keep $21.98. Since 21 September 2026 the 8% drops once you sell $15,000 or more in a four-week period.

The figures use Whatnot's US rate card as published on its help center (updated 1 October 2026), on the item price alone with no buyer-paid shipping or tax. Platforms change their fees, so check Whatnot's own fee page before you price anything.

Whatnot's fees in 2026

  • Commission: 8% of the item price for sellers under $15,000 in sales per four-week period (4% on Coins). Shipping and tax the buyer pays are not included.
  • Payment processing: 2.9% + $0.30 per order, charged on the total the buyer pays: item price, shipping and tax.
  • Lower tiers: sell more in a four-week period and your commission drops for the next period. Tiers reset every 28 days.
  • Cancellations: Whatnot may charge a fee if you cancel orders, so only list what you have in hand.

Older articles still quote a flat 8% for everyone. That changed on 21 September 2026, when Whatnot moved all sellers to sales-based tiers.

What you keep is not all yours

Reselling profit is usually taxable income, and the IRS taxes the profit, not the sale price. Many resellers move a fixed share of every payout, often 15% to 30%, into a separate account so April is not a shock. This is not tax advice. Ask a tax professional what rate fits you.
